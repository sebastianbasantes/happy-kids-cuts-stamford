About us

Our Story

It all began 22 years ago when I arrived in this wonderful country with a suitcase full of dreams and hopes. Today, we can proudly say that our salon, specialized in haircuts for children, has grown and transformed into a family space where parents, siblings, and grandparents eagerly come to A & G Happy Kids Haircuts.

Gloria and her team have managed to create a special and unique environment for children with special needs, as well as for those who are more sensitive or anxious about their first haircut. Over time, many of them have grown up and continue to be our most loyal customers.

I invite you to keep creating memories and new experiences with us. We appreciate all the recommendations that help us improve in what we do. Thank you!
