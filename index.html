About us

A salon kids actually love

For over a decade we've turned haircut day into the easy part. Our stylist Gloria and the team make every child feel comfortable — from nervous first-timers to teens who know exactly what they want.

We use an organic product line with safe ingredients, and we welcome children with special needs with the patience and care they deserve.

  • Friendly, kid-focused environment
  • Gentle, no-rush experience
  • Families & adults welcome too
  • English & Spanish spoken · Se habla español
